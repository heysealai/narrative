# My Ántonia run: a memory that is mostly about someone else

**June 11, 2026.** Third corpus: *My Ántonia* (Cather, Gutenberg #242;
81,273 words → 46 chunks of ~1,800 words). Same method as the other runs
(`crusoe-rebuild-run.md`): Sonnet subagents as harvester via the keyless CLI,
one consolidation step per chunk, 10 settling steps. Chosen as the inverse
stress test: the narrator (Jim Burden) is "the user," but the book — and
therefore the memory — is overwhelmingly *about another person*. Crusoe
stresses the self-model; this stresses the people registry.

## Resulting graph

**54 distillants (45 registry / 9 profile), 311 leaves, 205 episodes.**
The registry did what the premise demanded: `people/antonia` became the
deepest structure in any corpus so far — four levels, organized by epoch and
by relationship:

```
people/antonia
├── shimerda-family (+ ambrosch)
├── antonia-at-harlings (+ harling-fit, dancing-crisis)
├── antonia-donovan-period
├── antonia-cuzak-family (+ cuzak-children, cuzak-farm-life)
└── jim-antonia-bond
    ├── shared-episodes
    └── bond-and-feeling (+ father-grief-thread)
```

Jim's own interiority — the book's other subject — landed in the profile:
`temperament/inner-life/mythologizing-nebraska/{nebraska-as-homeland,
protecting-memory}` ("patria," the plough against the sun, the twenty-year
avoidance he calls "perhaps cowardice").

Readings:

- **The facet probes that routed mechanically on Crusoe missed here — and
  that is corpus-honest.** Crusoe *names* his regrets and trusts; Jim Burden
  narrates love and grief without ever saying "regret" or "trust," so no
  line carries those families. The derivation can only advertise the
  evaluative weight the lines actually state — descent recovered everything
  anyway. Facet recall quality is a property of the lines, which is a
  property of the corpus voice.
- The person-centric registry held up: every Ántonia-epoch question routed
  to the right sub-node directly, and the four-level subtree stayed
  navigable (no probe needed more than 2 opens).
- The frame-narrator introduction harvested cleanly as facts-about-Jim told
  by a friend (his railway work, his marriage) — no identity confusion.
